#### Based on the article you selected and the module content, how would you describe the current state of hyperparameter tuning in ML?

It's certainly evolving, my paper was about combining methods of hyperparameter tuning. Seems like we've now got the basic hyperparameter methods and we're now tuning the methods! 

Instead of solving how to tune, we're solving problems like:
- What happens if there's a delay in getting outputs / results, how can you keep on tuning in an efficient way whilst waiting on our output
- How can we optimise cost / compute associated with proper optimisation?
- How can we combine methods and get the best out of all of them.


#### Looking ahead, where do you think research in hyperparameter tuning is headed?
It's such a big topic, because good hyperparameter tuning directly impacts the success of your model and output. But it requires so much compute to do it properly. 

So i think we'll continue to get innovative methods like the batch optimisation methods and local penalisation methods Ruth talked about in her video. Or the Various Ensemble Bayesian Optimization Strategy talked about in the scientific paper.

The research is moving from just how to we tune to how do we get the best / optimal performance of current methods knowing that sometimes compute, outputs, time are sometimes limited.
